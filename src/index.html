DEMO RECORDING — AUTOPLAYS ON HOVER

9anouni AI

A sovereign bilingual (Arabic & French) legal RAG platform engineered for high-precision statutory retrieval across 1,114 Tunisian legal code articles. Built to address the critical flaws of standard RAG systems in legal domains—specifically temporal inconsistency when statutes are amended and LLM hallucinations during citation.

System Architecture

Employs a bitemporal PostgreSQL 16 engine utilizing btree_gist exclusion constraints to track both assertion time (when the law was codified) and valid time (when the law was legally active), enabling exact point-in-time statutory historical time-travel without database downtime.

Key Engineering Highlights
  • ▹ Hybrid Retrieval: Fused BM25 sparse keyword matching and dense ChromaDB semantic embeddings via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF), scoring 95.2% Recall@5 with under 50ms retrieval latency.
  • ▹ Defensive Grounding: Implemented a strict defensive grounding pipeline powered by Google Gemini, eliminating hallucinations and ensuring 100% verifiable citations to primary legal sources.
  • ▹ Multilingual Document OCR: Ingests complex scanned legal gazettes and court decisions using an optimized OpenCV preprocessing and Tesseract OCR pipeline.
